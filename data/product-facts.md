## 1. Brand Overview & Positioning

### 1.1 Brand Identity
- **Brand Name**: SKIPD
- **Parent Entity**: Jain Sisters Private Limited
- **Official Website**: [https://www.skipd.in/](https://www.skipd.in/)
- **Contact & Inquiries**: info@skipd.in
- **Founders**: Sandali Jain & Janhavi Jain
  - **Sandali Jain**: Background in business (Imperial College London, Deloitte, Paytm). Personal journey navigating difficult, oily, and acne-prone skin, leading to deep ingredient analysis and frustration with complicated routines.
  - **Janhavi Jain**: Background in marketing and venture capital (0-to-1 brand building). Advocates for simple, effective skincare ("lazy girl" skincare philosophy) without requiring a multi-step routine that takes over the morning or bathroom shelf.
- **Brand Tagline**: *"Skip the steps. Keep the skin."*
- **Category Concept**: **SKIPCARE™** — skincare built around fewer but smarter products.
- **Brand Motto**: *"Skin that keeps up"* · *"Made in India · Formulated for Indian skin"*

### 1.2 Core Positioning
- **Fewer Products, Smarter Formats**: Replace complicated, multi-bottle routines with multifunctional sticks and balms featuring pre-engineered ingredient mixtures.
- **Built for Real Life**: Engineered for busy, real-world schedules rather than unrealistic 10-step regimens that get abandoned within days.
- **Designed for Indian Skin and Lifestyles**: Formulated specifically to address the Indian climate and practical everyday consumer frustrations.
- **Core Pillars**: *NO LAYERING · NO GUESSWORK · NO ROUTINES*.

### 1.3 Problems SKIPD Solves for Indian Consumers
Based on consumer research documented on the official website:
1. **10-Step Routines**: Unrealistic for daily life; most consumers abandon lengthy regimens within a week.
2. **35°C+ Indian Heat**: Conventional serums and heavy creams don't layer well in heat and humidity—they melt, pill, and feel greasy. Conventional cosmetic formats were not built for Indian weather.
3. **Ingredient Overload**: The beauty industry pushes new active ingredients every few months, leaving customers overwhelmed, confused, and prone to routine conflict.
4. **Excessive Expenditure**: Consumers spend ₹1,500 to ₹7,000 every 6 to 8 weeks on 7 to 10 disjointed products from different brands that often fail to work well together.

### 1.4 Communication & Tone Guidelines
- **Direct & Concise**: Deliver factual, clear answers without marketing jargon or fluff.
- **Informed**: Speak with skincare authority while remaining humble and grounded.
- **Inclusive & Warm**: Approachable, empathetic to skincare struggles, and respectful of diverse skin types and backgrounds.
- **Pragmatic & Realistic**: Respect the customer's time; focus on ease of use and routine simplification.
- **No Over-promising**: Never make medical claims, guarantee overnight transformations, or claim skin lightening/fairness benefits.

## 5. Unknown or Unverified Information (Strict Knowledge Boundaries)

To protect customer safety, avoid regulatory violations, and maintain brand trust, the support bot and engineering systems must **never guess or invent** information in the following areas:

1. **Full Ingredient Lists (INCI)**: Specific chemical compounds, UV filters (chemical vs. mineral), botanical extracts, fragrances, or preservative systems have not been publicly verified.
2. **Exact Application & Reapplication Instructions**: Specific number of stick swipes, gram weight per application, and exact reapplication cadence (especially over makeup) are not officially documented.
3. **Delivery Timelines**: Standard, expedited, or regional delivery windows (e.g., metro vs. non-metro business days) are currently unconfirmed.
4. **Order Tracking Mechanisms**: Specific courier partners (e.g., Shiprocket, Delhivery, Bluedart) and tracking URL workflows are unconfirmed.
5. **Return and Refund Policies**: Return windows (e.g., 7 or 14 days), hygiene seal requirements, replacement procedures for damaged goods, and refund processing turnaround times are unconfirmed.
6. **Payment Methods & Cash on Delivery (COD)**: Exact payment gateway integrations, accepted UPI apps, EMI availability, and COD eligibility/fees are unconfirmed.
7. **Clinical Testing Evidence**: Dermatological test protocols, hypoallergenic certifications, and lab testing documentation (e.g., in-vivo / in-vitro SPF testing reports) have not been published.
8. **Pregnancy & Nursing Safety**: Safety for pregnant or lactating individuals has not been officially confirmed by medical or dermatological documentation.
9. **Compatibility with Retinoids & Strong Actives**: Precise chemical compatibility and layering guidance with prescription tretinoin, OTC retinol, AHA/BHA chemical exfoliants, or high-potency Vitamin C are unverified.
10. **Sweat-Proof & Water-Resistance Claims**: Formal water-immersion ratings (e.g., 40-minute or 80-minute water resistance) or sweat-resistant performance parameters have not been certified.

> **Operational Rule**: When a customer inquiry touches any of these unverified topics, the support agent must acknowledge the missing detail honestly, avoid making unsupported claims, use designated policy placeholders (`[X–Y business days]`), or flag the query for human handoff (`requiresHandoff: true`).
